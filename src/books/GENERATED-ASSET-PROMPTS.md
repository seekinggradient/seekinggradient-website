# Generated illustration prompts

The original generation records were not colocated with these two source images. The prompts below are faithful normalized reconstructions of their visual intent, composition, constraints, and negative constraints, retained so future editions can reproduce or replace them responsibly.

## `09-data-refinery-illustration.png`

> Create a wide, hand-drawn editorial illustration of a web-data refinery on a warm off-white paper background. On the far left, an overwhelming heap and conveyor of messy multilingual web pages, documents, browser windows, diagrams, and media scraps feeds into a sequence of five connected industrial filter tanks. Each tank visibly removes a different class of debris into a wheeled bin while progressively cleaner pages continue through pipes. On the right, the cleaned documents become colorful token blocks, then neatly packed rectangular training batches on a conveyor. Use precise ink outlines, restrained blue, rust, mustard, and olive accents, subtle watercolor shading, and generous whitespace. The metaphor must read left to right without labels. No logos, no identifiable websites, no photorealism, no 3D-render gloss, no watermark, and no decorative text.

## `10-transformer-block-workshop-illustration.png`

> Create a wide, hand-drawn conceptual illustration of a Transformer block as a continuous mechanical workshop on a warm off-white paper background. A transparent residual-stream pipe carrying parallel colored ribbons runs horizontally across the entire image. On the left, several external source sockets send orange, blue, and yellow cables into one junction on the stream, representing information gathered across token positions. A clear break in the center separates stages. On the right, the same ribbons enter a larger cutaway feature-processing machine with expanded green and purple internal channels, switches, and connectors, then return to the unchanged-width residual pipe. Use precise ink outlines, muted industrial colors, subtle watercolor shading, and abundant whitespace. No labels, equations, logos, photorealism, futuristic neon, watermark, or ornamental background elements.
